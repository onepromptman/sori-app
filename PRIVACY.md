# Privacy

Sori decodes and transcribes audio on your device. The first transcription
downloads the speech runtime from jsDelivr and the Whisper model from
Hugging Face. Adding a podcast feed or fetching episode audio contacts
the publisher or media host. The local setup assistant may download a
portable runtime from Node.js to start Sori.

Pasting a YouTube transcript stores the text and video link in Sori.
If you share a browser tab, Sori records only its audio for local
transcription. The recording is temporary and stays in the browser.
Searching for a podcast sends the show name you enter to Apple's podcast
directory. Selecting a feed contacts that publisher.

Your sources, transcripts, and imported files stay in your browser
library. The library is tied to the browser profile and site address.
Clearing the site's browser data removes it. Download copies of your
transcripts and training files to keep outside the browser.

When you choose to submit a slide request to ChatGPT or Claude, that chat
receives the transcript. The chat provider handles it under your account's
settings. Sori stores finished files locally when you bring them back.
