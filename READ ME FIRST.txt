Open Sori from source
=====================

The standalone Mac and Windows downloads include Python.
This source ZIP requires Python 3.11 or newer.

1. Unzip the whole download.
2. Open the Sori folder and run START SORI.command on Mac
   or START SORI.bat on Windows.
3. Sori opens in your browser. Leave the launcher window open.

Choose Try a sample transcript or add a podcast RSS feed and click Transcribe.
To make slides, open a transcript, click Make slides, and paste the copied
request into ChatGPT or Claude. No chatbot is needed to open Sori or transcribe.

Use Settings > Stop to close Sori.
