Open Sori
=========

1. Unzip the whole download.
2. Open the Sori folder. On Mac, double-click START SORI.command.
   On Windows, double-click START SORI.bat (Windows Batch File).
3. Sori opens in your browser. Leave the launcher window open.

Choose Try a sample transcript or add a podcast RSS feed and click Transcribe.
The first launch downloads the speech model in the background if needed.
Python is included. Transcription works without an account or chatbot.

To make slides, open a transcript and click Make slides. Choose ChatGPT or
Claude and paste the copied request. The request includes your transcript.

Use Settings > Stop to close Sori. To reopen it, use the same launcher.
