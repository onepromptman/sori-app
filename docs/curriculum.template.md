# My learning curriculum

## Audience
Who is learning, and what do they already know?

## Goals
- A practical task I want to be able to complete.
- A decision I want to explain using evidence.

## Scope
Topics to include, topics to skip, and material I have permission to use.

## Practice
How long a session should take and the finished artifact I want to produce.

## Review
How I will check the exercise, source quotes and answers before relying on them.
