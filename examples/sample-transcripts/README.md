# Sample transcripts

These four original, fictional transcript examples demonstrate the sort of
material you can read, search and turn into a self-study lesson. They do not
come from a real podcast, speaker, customer, employer or personal recording.
They are written examples, not evidence of transcription accuracy.

Open any TXT file to read it without installing Sori. To see these files in
Sori's Library, create a folder named `Sori Samples` inside your transcript
folder and copy the four TXT files there. Find your transcript folder in
Settings. You can remove the sample copies later without affecting real work.

- 01: Explain an idea in your own words.
- 02: Check a claim against its source.
- 03: Turn a question into a small experiment.
- 04: Plan a practice session you can finish.

All examples are distributed under Sori's MIT license. Any suggested exercises
are learning activities, not claims about measured outcomes. Optional training
generation sends the selected transcript to your configured AI provider and
may incur API charges.
